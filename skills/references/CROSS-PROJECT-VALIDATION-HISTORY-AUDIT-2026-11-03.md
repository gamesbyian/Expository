# Cross-project historical audit: validation cost, misleading evidence and abandoned engineering approaches

**Research snapshot:** 2026-11-03 UTC. **Purpose:** provide a durable, source-linked historical basis for future improvement of development/testing Skills. **Scope:** documentation/research only; no Skills or product implementation is changed by this report.

## Executive conclusions

The archive does not support a universal rule to batch six features before testing, nor a universal rule to run the entire project after each change. It does document several independent recurring causes of wasted engineering effort: (1) heavyweight workflows dispatching when their executed modes cannot reach the changed behavior; (2) repeated expensive end-to-end diagnostics after the fault is localized to one later phase; (3) artifacts and nominally green gates that do not measure the claimed property; (4) unreliable or incomplete test harnesses hiding the first substantive failure; (5) invalid research controls, benchmark variance and untested assumptions prompting avoidable experiments; (6) duplicate build, publisher, artifact, or data-preparation work; (7) concurrent agents repeatedly invalidating or cancelling each other's CI; and (8) human-facing acceptance deferred despite real cross-environment emulation gaps.

Some additional validation caught expensive mistakes and was worth its cost. The clearest cases are UR-Recomp's 76 false-negative Bash assertions, Pathfinder's invalid work budgets, AYA's synthetic release-proof booleans, Ring Road's actual handset rendering defects and ROYGBIV's failed-to-replicate performance gains. Removing such controls to increase short-term throughput would be counterproductive.

**A result's informativeness, its actual cost, and its eligibility to qualify a release are three distinct things.** This report records those distinctions rather than multiplying test counts into imagined productivity gains.

## Method, evidence levels and coverage

Sources: GitHub issues and PRs, their exact-head acceptance reports, workflow/job records and selected underlying job logs, plus the already source-linked project casebooks. Direct job-log inspection exists for the two Expository red Android runs, Chunks stalled reboot and the Skills PR's CI dispatches. For other cases, dated PR/issue bodies are **project-maintained secondary evidence**; linked artifact checksums and reported run IDs permit later independent audit. Merged means the source change was integrated, not necessarily the physical/device/business outcome verified. Closed-unmerged experiments are separately identified. Some older PR bodies include explicitly superseded progress checkpoints; the latest disposition takes precedence.

Coverage includes the accessible repositories [Chunks](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks), [Expository](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Expository), [Glimpse](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Glimpse), [UR-Recomp](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp), [wallgen / ROYGBIV](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen), [AYA](https://github.com/gamesbyian/AYA), [Pathfinder-Game](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Pathfinder-Game), [Ring-Road](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Ring-Road), [GRA-EDISNI](https://github.com/gamesbyian/GRA-EDISNI), [S60](https://github.com/gamesbyian/S60), [general-purpose](https://github.com/GBIDEVELOPMENT/general-purpose) and a survey of seven smaller game/archive repositories. Queries searched older and newer PRs/issues, not just currently open ones, using validation/CI/harness/timeout/measurement terms and dated histories. This is a **broad, categorized retrospective, not an exhaustive line-by-line audit of every PR or Actions job** in these large repositories. The most consequential documented wrong turns are indexed below.

### 1. Chunks: engine, storage, remote transport, CI and Android

- **CH01: misguided custom disk-writer premise.** [Issue #568](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/568), [#573](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/573), [#755](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/755), [#864](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/864). A blanket “same-name files must never be overwritten” test conflicted with the owner's conventional BitTorrent exact-path reuse policy. Several stacked admission/custom-writer drafts arose before existing libtorrent disk behavior and actual prior Android Downloads tests were reconciled. #568 was closed **superseded, not passed**; bespoke writer candidates were closed unmerged. A normal same-relative-path regular payload remains allowed, while unrelated files and app-private provenance remain protected. **Speed impact:** substantial branch/review churn is visible, but hours and avoided future time are unmeasured. Do not rewrite this as evidence that real unsafe ZIP/SAF overwrites are acceptable.
- **CH02: Android foundation dispatched for instrumentation-only edits.** [#878](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/878). Identified via full workflow path census and 100-run timing sample; a broad app/** trigger launched 6–9-minute foundation builds even for androidTest-only source already built and tested in API35 native instrumentation. Ordered exclusion plus mixed-change positive controls were merged. **Impact:** 385-s median baseline; 1→0 redundant foundation dispatch predicted for qualifying edits, post-change run savings not established.
- **CH03: silent or misleading suite success.** [#879](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/879), [#884](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/884). A green job could have empty/skipped/malformed JUnit; installed-test reports were not necessarily counted. Added opt-in fail-closed executed-test parser, then 68-workflow automated trigger/ownership inventory. **Impact:** correctness of CI receipts improved; no proven cycle-time saving.
- **CH04: anonymous public RPC mistaken for valid supplied credentials.** [#891](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/891), [#898](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/898). Challenge-less version/inventory reads could yield a false authenticated marker. First corrected *test harness only*; follow-up corrected the **shipping Connect callback**. Strong example where additional tests without tracing the real caller did not close the product flaw. One #891 JVM suite took **5m21s**, 1,168 executed + 30 skipped. Impact on development speed not quantified.
- **CH05: successful real peer transfer tested the wrong API.** [#903](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/903). The genuine disposable rTorrent/libtorrent byte test observed global/one-off traffic but bypassed production per-hash readTrafficSample. Acceptance was changed to assert exact-infohash monotonic received and verified samples through the shipping method. **Impact:** meaningful coverage improvement, not a measured time saving.
- **CH06: false resume failure from an obsolete test metric.** [#869](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/869), [issue #9](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/9). API35 run #41246130576 failed after 321 executed/16 skipped because the test demanded fresh saveResumeData callbacks for a valid already-persisted magnet. Corrected to inspect the actual immutable persisted generation and reran exact head; real byte/restart/seeding assertions remained. **Impact:** avoided changing production durability to satisfy an invalid callback-count oracle; no retrospective time sum.
- **CH07: bad starting fixture disguised missing peer traffic.** [Issue #1041](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/1041), [#1051](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/1051), [merged #1124](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/1124). Real Seedhost magnet showed received=0 but verified=735,358,976 bytes, likely already present. Initial rotation draft closed unmerged; later #1124 integrated magnet fixture rotation. Two real API35 disposable runs #41559424992 and #41562219423 established distinct publisher magnets, genuine received/verified bytes, restart/peerless and no deletion of old control payload. **Impact:** prevents expensive wait on invalid fresh-ingress fixture; no measured aggregate saved duration. .torrent/API29/protected host remain separate.
- **CH08: chained real lifecycle job stalls silently.** [Run #41547036035](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/actions/runs/41547036035), [#1123](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/1123). Real foreground-service timeout and cold restore passed before reboot armed; the overall job timed out at ~35m. The ADB wait is a plausible but unproven root cause. Added bounded command budgets and timestamps. **Impact:** observed lost 35m run, but no demonstrated post-fix reboot success in this historical record.
- **CH09: Compose lifecycle crash confused with feature test outcome.** [Issue #901](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/901). API35 native Add suite crashed after roughly 48 tests in SlotTable disposal; foundation, R8 and atlas were green. Actual app regression and #899 causality unproven. Isolated test/neighbor batch/fixture order proposed instead of blind rerun. **Impact:** no confirmed fix or duration savings.
- **CH10: synthetic UI and actual app/host coverage diverged.** [Issue #9](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/9), [#82](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/82), [#992](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/992). Production MainActivity read-only real UI integration uncovered tall-card interaction defects that parser/model tests could not; a 200%-font multi-viewport campaign later remained an unmerged proposal. **Impact:** avoided premature everyday-use declarations; no speed metric.
- **CH11: conflated asynchronous UI wait.** [#860](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/860). A single 5-s condition mixed Dispatchers.IO callbacks and Compose rendering after 1,178 JVM cases had already passed. Test-only change separated exact callback invocations and rendered statuses, retaining negative assertions. **Impact:** clearer failure localization; no post-fix flake-rate study.
- **CH12: many stale branches required integration reconciliation.** [#812](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/812), [#856](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/856), [#868](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/pull/868). Independent drafts with overlapping fixes and different base trees had old green checks. Unique source/tests were reconciled before final combined-head acceptance, rather than merging every old passing PR. **Impact:** repeated review/CI integration cost is real, but no measured counterfactual.
- **CH13: necessary full acceptance is still unresolved.** [Issues #5](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/5), [#569](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/569), [#809](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/809), [#1113](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/1113). ### 4. UR-Recomp: false-green native QA, Windows economics and science validity

A path-filtered light hygiene check did not run on changes needing its stable required context. ## Mechanisms and explicit counterexamples

1. **Unreachable tests / wrong mode.** UR science-only source under non-science Windows smoke; Glimpse render atlas's missing Reel; AYA same-UID provider testing; Chunks real peer bytes with production per-hash sampler bypassed.
2. **Unreliable acceptance oracle.** Chunks completed magnet, retired resume callback count, Expository stale heading, Pathfinder nominal work, AYA synthetic packet “confirmed”, Godot blank PNG, UR negative grep.
3. **Failing setup or instrumentation mistaken for product defect.** Expository system picker foreground, Chunks Compose teardown, ROYGBIV legacy Draw fixture, Godot engine exporter conflicts (in this case *real* product integration, so not to be waived).
4. **The expensive suite gives less new information than a narrow discriminator.** Expository post-byte Activity teardown, Chunks chained reboot, Night Court unavailable FTP before PyTorch, UR native scientific failure digest, Pathfinder precommit economic screening.
5. **Duplicate or poorly scheduled setup.** Chunks instrumentation-only foundation, UR SDL3/cache and three misrouted builds, AYA duplicate Godot job, Glimpse repeated Gradle across 14 workflows, ROYGBIV dual Pages, Night Court legacy 13-step pipeline.
6. **Unreliable experimental comparison.** ROYGBIV warm-run reversal, Pathfinder width500 ineligible policy, no-solve strict controls over 250k, Ring Road off-GPU CI vs real hardware. An experiment that falsifies an alluring optimization can be highly valuable even with zero production output.
7. **Batching counterexample.** A single gigantic chained job without per-phase timeouts can lose the entire remaining verification when reboot hangs. Batching should reuse setup, **not merge failure visibility**. Likewise, an unverified storage migration must not be allowed to underpin six dependent features before cheap early negative tests.
8. **Release/evidence counterexample.** Physical GPU, real third-party assistant recipient, protected-host mutation, actual OS picker permissions and data-preserving signer continuity cannot be earned by aggregating green simulated or source tests. Honest red is often cheaper than future user data loss.
