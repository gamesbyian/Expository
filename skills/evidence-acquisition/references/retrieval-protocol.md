# Retrieval protocol: concrete search, source and comparison records

**Input:** a consequential decision and unknown external facts. **Output:** independent source records and a defensible comparison. **Not an oracle:** a long bibliography, generated links, search-result snippets or apparent consensus.

## A. Question and universe

Record question ID, two materially different possible answers, action each would change, actor, product/version, geography and evidence window. Reject questions that cannot affect the next decision unless a general survey was expressly requested. Construct source-class slots: owner/primary specification, code/running product, actual user report, competitor manual, installed observation, original practitioner material, primary standard, and counterexample. Mark needed/optional/not applicable before retrieval.

## B. Generate query routes without guessing the market

Starting from user goal, generate and selectively execute: job language; problem/failure language; UI/control synonyms; technical and platform names; known competitors and alternatives; adjacent domain user jobs; source-code/package search; release history; praise and criticism; and relevant original-language practitioner expressions. Do not equate automatically translated terms with native language coverage. Log each executed query, engine/index/site, date, locale and candidate selection/rejection.

Use at least two origin-diverse routes for a consequential source class when reasonably available, e.g. manufacturer documentation and installed OSS implementation, or firsthand technician discussion and professional trade evidence. If the second route is not available, mark scope as COVERAGE LIMITED.

## C. Source and claim ledger

Source: ID | exact locator/URL/commit | original publisher/thread | source kind | language | date/version | inspected/not searched/inaccessible | mirror/lineage family | evidence limitations.

Claim: ID | source ID | exact span or executed interaction trace | proposition | version and actor | observation vs authored assertion vs interpretation | contradictory source IDs | status.

Inspect the original page/code/video/app when an important claim depends on it; search snippets nominate, never establish product behavior. Similar labels do not establish shared identity. Duplicates and translated reports share lineage unless the original accounts are demonstrably independent. Preserve quote licensing and do not republish lengthy excerpts.

## D. Behavior and user-reaction extraction

For each credible product/version compare user-journey outcomes: entry, selection, controls, visible status, empty/loading, permission denied, cancellation, retry/recovery, privacy/share, undo and accessibility where material. Status classes: UNKNOWN; NOT INSPECTED; NOT DOCUMENTED; AUTHOR CLAIM; USER REPORTED; OBSERVED IN RUN; REPRODUCIBLY ABSENT. Never fill UNKNOWN as NO.

For comments, distinguish firsthand account vs quotation/repost, praise/complaint, task and user situation, version, severity and sampled frequency; retain denominator only when the actual sampling method and inspected population are known. Forum votes are not a representative preference poll.

Evidence ladder: D0 discovered name/snippet; D1 first-party documented behavior; D2 witnessed third-party report; D3 personally executed and recorded running behavior; D4 independently repeated across materially different conditions. Each tier supports narrower claims than population prevalence.

## E. Challenge and stopping

Hold out an independent source family or choose a contrary source before reading the final synthesis. Reverse-search from failure, nonuser exit or another industry. Track newly learned concepts, contradicted cells, new user cohorts and different viable solutions per retrieval round. A round with zero new material can justify stopping **only when** still-untested source classes and cheap counterexamples are unlikely to change the next decision; explicitly state that reasoning.

Deliver source-backed alternatives, unknowns, exact scope, decision implications, next cheap discriminating test and, if human preference matters, an optional [choice handoff](../../human-agent-interaction-engineering/references/opportunity-handoffs.md). For complex relations, pass staged evidence to [source ingestion](../../concept-model-engineering/references/source-ingestion-reconciliation.md).

## Negative controls

Known project label fix: no browsing. Ten copied press releases: one origin. Unsuccessful English search: no absence claim until alternative terminology/versions considered. Button screenshot: only visual appearance proven. Three vocal complaints: describe sample, never all users.
