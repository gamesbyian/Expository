**Hypothetical; no external queries or product tests have been executed.** These are research instructions, not Retrofit requirements or discovered facts.

**Question:** Should an initial mobile workflow use manually placed annotations or automatic equipment masks? Alternative outcomes change UI, external-service cost, editing data and recovery; inquiry qualifies.

**Source classes:** Android platform grants/graphics APIs; mature markup apps documented or code-inspected; field/facility inspection workflows; original professional complaints and praise; accessibility; model SDKs for separate resource-discovery handoff.

**Query routes:** job "mark equipment on maintenance room photograph"; task "annotate inspection photo and export"; technical "Android image overlay annotation undo"; competitor "field inspection app photo annotation"; adjacent insurance-contractor snag list; failure "annotation lost after reopen"; selected native-language facility terminology only where real practitioners warrant.

**Uninspected matrix fields:** target placement, undo, source-photo preservation, history, offline, accessibility, permission loss, opaque masks, explicit external recipient and prompt handoff. Leave UNKNOWN until real evidence is read.

**Disconfirmation:** an existing maintained library may already provide all necessary editable mask operations; this would weaken a build-from-scratch claim. Or real user reports might show manual markers are unacceptable for occluded equipment. Either is a target to investigate, not an invented fact.
