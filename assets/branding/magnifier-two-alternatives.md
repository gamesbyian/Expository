# Two magnifier alternatives

Both independent, editable SVGs derive from [the gray-folder composition](magnifier-four-layer-half-spacing-pastel-lens-gray-folders.svg). The source remains unchanged.

## [Disk stack only](magnifier-pastel-disk-only.svg)
Retains the original storage cylinder's vector paths, colours, tier dots and exact -11° tilt, but removes all folders and dotted connectors. The stack is enlarged by a factor of 1.5 around its original centre in the lens layout and translated to the centre of the lens. The original circular clip trims the enlarged disk at the top and bottom. Fine-tune `#centered-cylinder` to adjust its crop and placement without redrawing anything. The white magnifier and the existing rotated pastel echoes are unchanged.

## [Parallel echoes](magnifier-pastel-parallel-echoes.svg)
Keeps the complete black-lens composition, storage, folders, dotted connectors and white magnifier unchanged. Removes rotation from only the red, blue, and yellow `<use>` elements, retaining their original diagonal offsets of (69.12, 69.12), (138.24, 138.24), and (207.36, 207.36) SVG units. All four magnifying-glass handles are now parallel.

No embedded raster data and no live app/icon replacement.
